Privacy

We collect only what the service needs.

What we collect

For found IDs: the name on the ID, its last 5 characters, the place found, and the finder's name and phone. For lost IDs: the name, last 5 characters and your WhatsApp number. For claims: your name and WhatsApp number. We never store photos of IDs or full ID numbers, and we do not record the number of the alternative ID shown at handover.

What the public sees

Only a partly hidden name, the last 5 characters and the town. Phone numbers and exact places are visible to authorised staff only.

How long we keep it

Completed or rejected found reports are deleted after {{ RETENTION_FOUND }} days. Lost registrations are deleted after {{ RETENTION_LOST }} days once closed or matched. To ask for earlier deletion, contact us{% if SITE_CONTACT_EMAIL %} at {{ SITE_CONTACT_EMAIL }}{% endif %}.
